## Scope
Read-only PostgreSQL catalog audit of tables in `public`. It reports disabled RLS, enabled RLS with no policies, policies applying to PUBLIC, and table-level INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE grants to anon/authenticated. A broad policy or grant is a **review finding**, not automatically an exploitable vulnerability; RLS conditions and function privileges still matter.
